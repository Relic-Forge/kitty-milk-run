# Kitty Milk Run — Art Direction

## Style

Blocky, bright, retro, kid-made-in-the-best-way.

The game should feel like a toy version of a racetrack. Everything should read clearly at a glance.

## Palette

- Grass: bright greens
- Track: warm dirt brown
- Cat: orange, cream, gray, black, or pink unlocks later
- Yarn: pink, purple, blue
- Milk: white with soft blue shadow
- Dogs: brown and friendly-looking
- Cucumbers: silly bright green

## Shape Language

- Cat: chunky rectangle body, triangle ears, big eyes
- Dog: blocky rectangle body, square head, waggy tail later
- Cucumber: long rounded green shape with stripes
- Yarn: circle with crossing lines
- Milk: big readable bottle or carton

## Animation Notes

V1 placeholder animations:

- Cat bob while running
- Cat spin/shake when hit
- Yarn pop on collection
- Milk cutscene bounce

Future animation ideas:

- Cat tail wiggle
- Dog bark pop-up
- Cucumber scare burst
- Yarn trail speed boost
- Milk sparkle celebration
